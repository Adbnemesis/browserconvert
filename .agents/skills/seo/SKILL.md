# Unified SEO Master Suite

This project includes a comprehensive suite of best-in-class SEO skills:

## 1. Technical & Foundation SEO
- **`web-quality-seo`** (by Addy Osmani): Google-standard web quality, meta tags, Open Graph, mobile optimization, and Core Web Vitals.
- **`seo-sitemap`** (by Agrici Daniel): Complete XML sitemap creation, validation, and robots.txt setup.
- **`core-web-vitals`** (by Addy Osmani): Lab and field optimization for LCP, INP, and CLS.

## 2. Audits & Diagnostics
- **`seo-audit-marketing`** (by Corey Haines): Deep technical SEO audit, indexing diagnosis, title tag optimizations, local SEO, rankings push.
- **`seo-audit-anthropic`** (by Anthropic): Strategic audit covering keyword opportunities, content gaps, competitor benchmarking, and quick-win prioritization.
- **`claude-seo`** (by Agrici Daniel): End-to-end full-site and single-page SEO auditing with CWV thresholds and EEAT analysis.

## 3. AI Search, Answer Engines & GEO
- **`ai-seo`** (by Corey Haines): Positioning for LLM-based search (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Gemini, Copilot).
- **`seo-geo`** (by OPC Skills): Generative Engine Optimization with Python scripts for SERP analysis, keyword research, and schema generation.
- **`seo-aeo-best-practices`** (by Sanity): Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), JSON-LD schemas, and EEAT structuring.

## 4. Content & Growth
- **`seo-content-writer`** (by Aaron Zhu): Drafting high-intent, snippet-targetable content and refreshing decaying pages.
- **`seo-content`** (by Agrici Daniel): Content usefulness, E-E-A-T evaluation, thinness detection, and AI copy cleanup.
- **`seo-competitor-pages`** (by Agrici Daniel): High-converting "X vs Y" comparison pages and alternatives directories.
- **`programmatic-seo`** (by Corey Haines): Building scalable template × data SEO page systems.
- **`search-console-seo`** (by Ian Nuttall): Data-driven queries, Search Console data, and ranking analysis.
